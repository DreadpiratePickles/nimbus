Methodology & scope

Findings were collected agentlessly by cloud-posture (native collectors plus Prowler, ScoutSuite, Checkov, Trivy and Steampipe adapters), normalised to canonical check IDs and stored in SQLite. Each finding maps to AICPA SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria controls through (1) the canonical catalog's explicit control mappings, (2) weakness-derived mappings (each finding's weakness vocabulary, e.g. NO_MFA, maps to controls).

  • FAIL: at least one failing finding maps to the control.
  • PASS: only passing checks map to the control.
  • NOT ASSESSED: no automated check covers the control; collect manual evidence.
  • Score = passing ÷ (passing + failing) controls = 18.1%.
